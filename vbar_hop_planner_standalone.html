Per hop: Δvx = −2n(1−cos ψ)/D·Δy, Δvy = n sin ψ/D·Δy with ψ = nΔt and D = 8(1−cos ψ) − 3ψ sin ψ; the stopping burn has the same magnitude. A hop is passively safe when ψ < π: a missed stop then drifts the chaser up and away from the target. Total ΔV is the per-metre cost times the distance, so hop count and γ change time and safety margin, not fuel.
